What does a project with Ascent look like?

It starts with a no-pressure discovery call. We build a game plan, run the full production, then deliver cut-downs for every platform.

Do you only film outdoor and adventure brands?

No. Adventure is our home turf, but we shoot lifestyle, hospitality, events and product for any brand that wants content people actually watch.

Can you keep content coming after the first shoot?

Yes — our ongoing retainer keeps fresh content flowing so you stay visible between campaigns.

Where are you based?

Burleigh Heads, QLD, Australia — and we travel for shoots on land, on water and in the air.

How much does it cost?

Every package is built around your goals. Book a call and we'll scope it together.

What do you need from us before a shoot?

A clear goal, your audience and any must-have shots. We handle concept, locations and permits from there.
