[ FAQ ]

Before you ask.

The six things clients ask on every first call.

What does a project with Ascent look like?

It starts with a no-pressure discovery call. We build a game plan, run the full production, then deliver cut-downs for every platform.

Do you only film outdoor and adventure brands?

No. Adventure is our home turf, but we shoot lifestyle, hospitality, events and product for any brand that wants content people actually watch.

Can you keep content coming after the first shoot?

Yes. Many clients move onto a monthly retainer — we plan shoots ahead and deliver a steady stream of platform-ready cuts.

Where are you based?

Burleigh Heads on the Gold Coast, QLD. We travel Australia-wide, and further for the right project.

How much does it cost?

Every project is scoped to your goals and channels. Book a call and we'll send a clear, itemised quote.
